# QA Analyst Report — v1 Walkthrough Videos

**Verdict: NEEDS WORK**

Reviewed all four videos frame-by-frame (ffmpeg frame extraction at ~15–25s intervals, 54 frames viewed), cross-checked every on-screen bubble against the transcript JSONs under `work/*/transcripts/`, read all narration scripts, verified audio presence per video with ffprobe, and spot-checked claims against the repos (`~/workspace/biteflow`, `~/workspace/roomlens`). **Method note on "real engine output":** the drivers (`drive_journey.py`, `drive.py`) import the repos' real `process_incoming` state machines via the FakeWhatsAppClient pattern the repos' own tests use, and frames are rendered from those transcripts. The failures below are in what the narration *claims*, what the manifest *promises*, what the captions *cover up*, and what the demo media *pretends to be*.

## BLOCKER

### B1 — RoomLens "room photo" and "chair cutout" are synthetic placeholders presented as real
**Videos:** `roomlens-prospect-mr.mp4` (~01:00, 01:40, 02:00), `roomlens-designer-en.mp4` (~01:15, 02:05)
**Expectation violated:** rubric #1 (frames genuinely real engine output) + basic demo honesty.

The compositing pipeline is real (`src/composite.py` did run), but **both inputs are fake**:
- The "room photo" the prospect sends is `viz/room-a.jpg` / `assets/prospect-room-photo.jpg` — pure gray **noise** (verified by opening the source file; 800×600 RGB static, generated by `make_test_room(seed=...)` in `drive.py:94`).
- The "Aria Chair" is `make_placeholder_cutout("Aria Chair")` — a brown rounded rectangle with the text "Aria Chair" on it, not a product photo.

Meanwhile the narration asserts a real photo at every turn: prospect-mr Step 3 says "आता खोलीचा एक स्पष्ट फोटो पाठवायचा" (send a clear photo of your room) and Step 5 says "तुमच्याच खोलीत एरिया चेअर" (the chair *in your own room*); designer-en Step 4 says the prospect "sends a photo of their living room" and Step 5 says the engine "pastes the chair cutout onto the real room photo." The centerpiece full-screen visualization (prospect-mr 02:00, designer-en 02:05) is a labeled brown rectangle on TV static. The closing cards say "preset compositing, not true AR" — but say **nothing** about the room and chair being placeholders. Anyone evaluating the product from this video is misled about what the demo actually demonstrates. Fix: use a real room photo + real product cutout, or add explicit on-screen/closing disclosure that demo media is synthetic.

Related sub-finding: prospect-mr Step 3 narration describes the photo quality gate ("अंधारातला किंवा धूसर फोटो असेल तर बॉट नव्याने फोटो मागतो; चुकीचं प्रीव्यू कधीच बनवलं जात नाही") — yet the video never demonstrates the retake path, and the demo's own noise image sails through `assess_quality` (noise has ~128 mean brightness and high Laplacian variance, so it passes). The narrated quality gate is never shown working.
