# Your first visualization

You're a customer. You message a furniture studio's WhatsApp number because
you want to see how a chair (or lamp, or shelf) would look in *your* room.
Here's exactly what happens — no app, no account, no typing required.

## Scene 1 — Say hello, pick your studio

Message the number. Reply `2` ("preview furniture"). You'll see a numbered
list of studios — reply with the number of the one you want. It then asks
for your name (so the designer sees your name instead of your phone number;
reply `0` to skip), and then for a photo of your room.

## Scene 2 — Take a good room photo

Photo tips (the difference between a preview today and a retake request):

- **Light it up.** Turn on every light in the room; daylight is best.
- **Step back.** Stand in the doorway so the whole wall or corner is visible.
- **Hold still.** A blurry photo gets a polite "please retake" — that's the
  app refusing to show you a misleading preview.
- **Send the original**, not a screenshot of a photo.

You can also send a **short video clip** — the first frame is used. If the
video can't be read, you'll be asked for a still photo instead.

## Scene 3 — What happens after you send

Your photo goes to the designer, who picks products and places them into your
photo. This takes a little while (a human is doing it). Meanwhile you'll be
asked once: reply `1` if you'd like occasional design tips and offers, `2` if
not. Replying `STOP` at any time opts you out of those messages forever.

## Scene 4 — You get the preview + price

You'll receive an image: your room with the furniture placed in it, plus a
price strip listing each product. Below it, four options:

```
1. Order it
2. Change the placement
3. Change the product
4. Talk to the designer
```

It's a **rough preview**, not a measurement — placement is illustrative, and
sizes are approximate. Options 2–4 go back to the designer with your note.

## Scene 5 — Order with a single digit

Reply `1`. You'll see the quote — line items, delivery, total. Reply `1` to
confirm 👍 (or `2` to cancel 👎). That's the order. Message the number anytime
afterward to check its status: received → preparing → shipped → delivered.

## Language

Send `language` (or `idioma` / `भाषा`) anytime to switch between English,
Spanish, Hindi, and Marathi. You'll pick up right where you left off.

Prices are shown in the currency of your language: ₹ for Marathi and Hindi,
$ for English and Spanish.
