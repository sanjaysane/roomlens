# Video script: First visualization (~5 min)

Presenter tone: friendly, slow-paced, aimed at a non-technical customer —
imagine explaining it to your parents.

---

## Scene 1 — What this is (0:00–0:40)

**On-screen:** a plain living-room photo; a sofa fades into it with a price
strip.

**Presenter:** "Want to see how a new sofa would look in your living room —
your actual room, not a showroom? With RoomLens you just use WhatsApp. No app
to install, no account, no forms. Let me show you."

## Scene 2 — Say hello (0:40–1:20)

**On-screen:** phone screen. Message the studio's number, welcome appears,
reply `2`, numbered studio list appears, reply `1`.

**Presenter:** "Message the studio's WhatsApp number. It asks what you'd like
to do — reply two, for 'preview furniture.' Pick the studio by its number.
Now it asks for a photo of your room."

## Scene 3 — Take a good photo (1:20–2:30)

**On-screen:** split screen — bad photo (dark, blurry) vs good photo (bright,
whole wall visible). Show the retake message on the bad one.

**Presenter:** "Here's the part that matters. Turn on all the lights —
daylight is best. Stand in the doorway so the whole wall is in the picture,
and hold the phone steady. If the photo is too dark or blurry, the app will
politely ask you to retake it instead of showing you a misleading preview.
That's on purpose. You can also send a short video clip — it uses the first
frame."

## Scene 4 — Wait for the magic (2:30–3:10)

**On-screen:** "preview being prepared" message; the one-time opt-in question
appears (`1` yes / `2` no).

**Presenter:** "Send the photo and wait a bit — a real designer is placing
the furniture by hand. You'll get one question: reply one if you'd like
occasional design tips and offers, two if not. And remember, sending the word
STOP anytime stops those messages for good."

## Scene 5 — The reveal (3:10–4:10)

**On-screen:** the preview image arrives — room with sofa, price strip. The
four options listed below it.

**Presenter:** "And here's your room — with the sofa in it, and the price
right on the image. Underneath, four choices: one to order, two to ask for a
different placement, three for a different product, four to talk to the
designer. Keep in mind it's a rough preview, not an exact measurement — the
designer will confirm the details."

## Scene 6 — Order with one digit (4:10–4:50)

**On-screen:** reply `1`, quote appears (line items, delivery, total), reply
`1` again — order confirmed with order number.

**Presenter:** "Reply one to order. You get the full quote — items, delivery,
total. Reply one again to confirm, with a thumbs-up, or two to cancel. Done.
Message the number anytime to check where your order is."

## Scene 7 — Close (4:50–5:00)

**On-screen:** recap: photo → preview → one digit.

**Presenter:** "Photo, preview, one digit. And if you ever want to switch
languages, just send the word 'language' — English, Spanish, and Hindi are
all supported."
