# Video script: Designer onboarding (~5 min)

Presenter tone: warm, practical, like showing a colleague over coffee.

---

## Scene 1 — The pitch (0:00–0:45)

**On-screen:** phone mock showing a WhatsApp chat; a room photo becomes a
room photo with a chair composited in, price strip visible.

**Presenter:** "RoomLens lets interior designers sell furniture entirely over
WhatsApp — no app for the customer to install, no website, no forms. Your
customer sends a photo of their room, you place your products into it with a
few numbered replies, they see the preview with prices, and they order with a
single digit. Today I'll take you from zero to your first sent visualization."

## Scene 2 — Becoming a designer (0:45–1:30)

**On-screen:** live phone screen. Message the RoomLens number, welcome menu
appears, reply `1`.

**Presenter:** "Message the RoomLens number and reply one — 'I'm a designer.'
Type your studio name once. That's the only typing in the whole setup flow.
You land in the designer hub: Catalog, Studio, Orders, Marketing. Everything
from here is numbered menus."

## Scene 3 — Building the catalog (1:30–2:45)

**On-screen:** Catalog → Add. Type name, type price, reply `1` for floor size,
send a product cutout PNG.

**Presenter:** "Reply one for Catalog, one for Add. Name the product, type
the price, pick a size class — floor, wall, or tabletop — with a digit. Then
send your product photo with the background removed. That's important: we
don't do background removal, so use any background remover first, or the
cutout pastes with its background. Reply zero to skip, and repeat for each
product."

## Scene 4 — The studio: placing a product (2:45–4:00)

**On-screen:** Studio shows a pending room photo. Pick it, pick the chair by
number, pick the `floor-center` preset, tap bigger once, send.

**Presenter:** "When a customer sends a room photo, you get a ping. Open
Studio, pick the room, pick the product, pick a placement preset — floor
center, left wall, right wall, or wall hang. Then adjust: bigger, smaller,
restart, or send. A quick honesty note for your customers: this is a 2D
preview, not augmented reality. Tell them it's a rough idea of the look, not
a measurement."

## Scene 5 — The order lands (4:00–4:45)

**On-screen:** order notification with total. Orders → pick order → `1`
preparing.

**Presenter:** "When the customer confirms, you get an order with the total.
Open Orders, pick it, and walk the status forward — preparing, shipped,
delivered — with single digits. The customer can check status just by
messaging the number."

## Scene 6 — Close (4:45–5:00)

**On-screen:** recap card: no app install → numbered menus → honest previews.

**Presenter:** "That's RoomLens: your catalog, their room, one chat thread.
Campaigns and win-backs live under Marketing — but only for customers who
opted in, and STOP always opts them out. Links to the setup docs are below."
